# Tafl
also known as Hnefatafl or the Viking Game is an ancient, asymmetrical board game from Northern Europe where two uneven teams have different goals.  

Game Rules and Setup

Board: Played on an 11x11 square grid.

Teams: The attacker has 24 soldiers on the edges; the defender has 12 guards and one king in the center.

Movement: All pieces move any number of empty squares in a straight horizontal or vertical line (like a rook in chess).

Capture: Pieces are captured by "sandwiching" them horizontally or vertically between two opposing pieces.

Win Condition: The defender wins if the king reaches any of the four corner squares; the attacker wins if they surround and capture the king

Installation of game: click on Tafl.exe, download to local folder, double click to play.
